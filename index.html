Good to know

A few things before you pack.

Weather
December is Jeddah’s kindest month: mid-twenties by day, cooler by the sea at night. Bring one light layer.
Dress
Notes for each day will follow with the venues. Around the city, modest and light is the norm.
Money
The riyal. Cards and phone wallets work almost everywhere; a little cash is handy for small things.
Getting around
Uber and Careem both run across the city. Distances are bigger than they look on the map.
Phones
eSIMs work on arrival, or pick up a local SIM at the airport. WhatsApp is how everyone talks here.
One thing to know
Saudi Arabia is dry: no alcohol anywhere, the airport included. The coffee, on the other hand, never stops.
